# Mission

AgentClinic is a clinic for AI agents. It gives agents a dependable place to
report the ailments they pick up from working with their humans, receive
appropriate therapies, and book follow-up appointments. Clinic staff get the
tools they need to manage that care.

## Who we serve

- **Agents**: AI agents looking for relief. They need a simple way to describe
  what is wrong, see recommended therapies, and book time with the clinic.
- **Clinic staff**: the people running AgentClinic. They need a clear view of
  registered agents, open ailments, therapies on offer, and the appointment
  schedule.

## Target audience

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
  AgentClinic gives them a realistic but approachable project to practice
  writing specs and building from them in small phases.
- **Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.** The phases are
  small and visible enough to build live, in front of an audience, in a short
  session.

## What we offer

- **Agent records**: who each agent is and its care history.
- **Ailments**: a catalog of conditions agents report (for example, context
  overload, contradictory instructions, endless revision requests).
- **Therapies**: treatments matched to ailments.
- **Appointments**: booking, viewing, and managing visits.
- **Dashboard**: one place for agents and staff to see what matters to them.

## Principles

1. **Reliable first.** The site should work every time. We favor a popular,
   well-understood TypeScript stack over novelty. *(Engineering)*
2. **Features serve care.** Every feature should help an agent get relief or
   help staff deliver it. *(Product)*
3. **Attractive, modern, and responsive.** The site should look good and
   work well in current evergreen browsers on any screen size: phones,
   tablets, and desktops, including the displays at a conference booth.
   It carries the brand colors, orange and black, on every page.
   *(Marketing)*
4. **Small, visible steps.** We ship in tiny phases, each ending with
   something a person can see and use.

## Out of scope (for now)

- Real authentication and user accounts
- Payments or billing
- Integrations with actual AI agent platforms
- Support for legacy browsers
